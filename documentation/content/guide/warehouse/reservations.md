# Reservations

Reservations let you allocate component stock for a purpose without removing
packets from the warehouse immediately.

## Create reservation

1. Open **Warehouse → Reservations**.
2. Create a new reservation and link the target component.
3. Set quantity and notes as required by your process.

## View and update

Open a reservation from the list to adjust quantity, status, or metadata.
Permissions follow the warehouse area access level for the signed-in user.
